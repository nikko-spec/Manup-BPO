OUR STORY

MAN UP started with a simple observation: good businesses were losing time and momentum to a problem that shouldn't be theirs to solve alone — finding and keeping the right people.

We saw companies stall out on hiring — burning weeks on job posts, screening, and no-shows, while the actual work piled up. So we built a manpower outsourcing company around one job: getting businesses the right people, at the right time, without the overhead.

Since then, that's stayed the whole point. Not a bigger service menu, not more buzzwords — just reliable manpower outsourcing solutions, delivered by a team that treats your staffing gap like our own problem to fix.
